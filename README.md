# Solmar City

An open-world crime game built with three.js for the make.fun GTA 6 bounty (gta6-01). Rico is back in Solmar — a sun-baked coastal city of seven districts — and the cartel wants him gone. Drive, ride, fly and sail your way from street hustler to Kingpin of Solmar.

## The city

Seven hand-built districts, each with its own look, roads, traffic and trouble: Downtown, Old Town, Little Havana, Sunset Beach, the Vineyards, North Hills and The Islands, plus a marina, an airfield and open ocean. A full day/night cycle and live weather roll over the city while traffic, police, pedestrians and your crew go about their business.

## What you can do

- **44 missions** across 11 arcs — from your first getaway to the M44 finale at the airfield, where you stop the cartel boss's plane and escape by seaplane to claim the crown.
- **Drive anything**: cars and Lowriders (H for hydraulics), motorcycles (Shift to wheelie), jet skis in the marina, boats, a seaplane, police and player helicopters — all with NOS boost on Shift.
- **Fight back**: fists, five weapons, sniper zoom on right-click, and a wanted system with police AI, helicopter pursuit and a Heat meter that never forgets.
- **Build an empire**: buy properties (B), collect protection money (E), run businesses, take over all six territories, fish off the Surf Shack pier, hit the casino, climb the racing league leaderboard, and manage your crew and garage (G).
- **Live the city**: six radio stations — including the beachy new **Sunset FM** — on C, M to mute, a 200×200 live minimap with roads, blips, territory tint and mission markers, and a full save system that keeps your cash, missions and stats.

## Controls

WASD move · Shift sprint / NOS · Space jump · F enter vehicle · Q punch · 1–5 weapons, R reload · C radio · M mute · P pause · B buy property · G garage · E interact / cast & reel while fishing · H hydraulics in a Lowrider · Right-click sniper zoom.

## Run it

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser (no build step, no network — three.js is vendored). Or serve the folder with any static server. The hand-in entrypoint is `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only): it copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to `/output` and writes `/output/dossier.json`.
